Weekly weigh-in

Every Saturday

Weigh yourself once a week, every Saturday morning after the bathroom and before eating. Each row compares the real change with what your logged calories predicted for that Saturday-to-Saturday week. Weight can swing 0.5–1 kg from water alone, so look at the trend over a few weeks.

Target weight

My week shows how many days it will take to reach this weight. That estimate uses your weigh-ins and the last 14 days of logs, and it refreshes every 3 days so day-to-day swings don't keep moving it. Changing your target or planned intake refreshes it straight away.

Don't know your resting burn? Estimate it

Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula × 1.2, which covers resting plus normal daily movement on a day with no workout.
